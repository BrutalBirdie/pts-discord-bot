## About

A Discord bot with a web UI to control it. Sign in with your Cloudron account.

* Tasks on a schedule (daily, weekly, once or cron, in any timezone), on Discord events (voice joins and leaves, new members, keywords in messages) or started by hand
* Task steps: join a voice channel or the channel a member is in, play a sound or a random one with a tag, send a message, send a direct message, wait, leave
* Slash commands in Discord: /play, /task, /stop and /leave
* Saved messages with text, up to 10 embeds, images and files, with a preview of how Discord shows them
* Media library for sounds, images, videos and files, uploaded one by one or as a zip, or added from a link, with tags. Sounds can be played in a voice channel right away
* Servers and channels the bot is in, with the permissions it is missing
* Run history per task. Tasks pause themselves on errors retrying cannot fix, and failures can be posted to a channel
* Admins change settings and see an audit log of all changes
